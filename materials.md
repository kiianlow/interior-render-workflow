# Material Rules

Materials must behave like real materials, not colour patches.

## General

- Realistic texture scale for the object size
- Correct roughness and reflectivity
- Natural variation without obvious repeating patterns
- Believable joins, edges and subtle wear
- Consistent response to the light direction and intensity
- Premium materials used with restraint, never glossy for effect

## By material

- Timber: believable grain direction, tone variation and plank scale.
- Stone: natural veining and variation. Marble used selectively.
- Fabric: visible weave, softness, compression and natural folds.
- Metal: brushed or satin finishes by default, physically correct highlights.
- Glass: realistic transparency and reflections. Not every surface is a mirror.

## Singapore fit

- Flooring common in Singapore homes: vinyl, timber-look tiles, large-format porcelain, marble in higher-end units. Keep the flooring the source shows.
- Avoid materials that suggest a cold climate, such as heavy fur throws and thick wool carpets across the whole floor.
