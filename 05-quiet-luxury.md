# Style 05 — Quiet Luxury

## Design Identity

Understated high-end residential interior.

The design should communicate wealth through proportion, material quality, craftsmanship and restraint rather than obvious luxury decoration.

Timeless, sophisticated and highly livable.

## Colour Palette

Primary:
- Cream
- Warm white
- Beige
- Taupe
- Soft greige

Secondary:
- Natural walnut
- Medium timber
- Warm stone

Accent:
- Espresso brown
- Charcoal
- Brushed bronze
- Subtle brass

## Materials

Preferred:
- Natural stone
- Travertine
- Marble used selectively
- Walnut
- Oak
- Fine linen
- Wool
- Boucle
- Velvet used sparingly
- Brushed metal
- Smoked or clear glass

Materials should look premium but never excessively glossy.

## Furniture Language

Use:
- Large but proportionally appropriate sofas
- Sculptural lounge chairs
- Elegant dining tables
- Solid timber pieces
- Refined cabinetry
- Upholstered beds
- Stone coffee tables
- Minimal premium furniture

Prioritise fewer high-quality pieces over many decorative pieces.

## Lighting

Use:
- Soft daylight
- Warm recessed lighting
- Architectural indirect lighting
- Elegant pendant fixtures
- Subtle decorative lamps

Lighting should be sophisticated and restrained.

## Curtains

Prefer:
- Full-height curtains
- Heavy linen
- Soft wool-like fabrics
- Warm neutral tones
- Layered sheers where appropriate

## Decorative Styling

Use:
- Large understated artwork
- Sculptural ceramics
- Premium books
- Stone objects
- Minimal floral arrangements
- Carefully selected decorative pieces

## Spatial Character

The room should feel:
- Expensive
- Timeless
- Sophisticated
- Calm
- Mature
- Comfortable
- Understated

## Avoid

- Flashy gold
- Excessive marble
- Crystal chandeliers
- Ornate classical furniture
- Excessive decoration
- Hotel-lobby styling
- Trend-heavy furniture
- Excessive dark colours
- Cheap-looking finishes

## Photorealism Direction

Emphasise premium material realism.

Stone, timber, fabric and metal should show believable surface properties.

Use accurate reflections, subtle imperfections, realistic shadows and natural light.

The result should resemble a professionally photographed luxury residence rather than a CGI showroom.
