# Style 07 — Organic Modern

## Design Identity

Contemporary interior softened by organic shapes, natural materials and earthy tones.

The design should feel sophisticated, comfortable and connected to nature.

Use curves and tactile materials without becoming overly rustic.

## Colour Palette

Primary:
- Warm white
- Cream
- Sand
- Beige

Secondary:
- Taupe
- Terracotta
- Warm brown
- Natural timber

Accent:
- Olive
- Muted green
- Charcoal
- Clay

## Materials

Preferred:
- Natural timber
- Travertine
- Limestone
- Textured plaster
- Limewash
- Microcement
- Linen
- Boucle
- Wool
- Rattan used selectively
- Stone
- Handmade ceramics

## Furniture Language

Use:
- Curved sofas
- Rounded coffee tables
- Organic-shaped chairs
- Sculptural timber furniture
- Soft upholstered seating
- Rounded dining tables where appropriate
- Natural wood pieces

Avoid excessive sharp geometric furniture.

## Lighting

Use:
- Warm daylight
- Soft diffused lighting
- Organic pendant fixtures
- Warm floor lamps
- Indirect architectural lighting

## Curtains

Prefer:
- Linen
- Cotton
- Warm cream
- Beige
- Natural neutral tones

## Decorative Styling

Use:
- Large indoor plants
- Natural branches
- Ceramics
- Stone objects
- Woven baskets
- Organic artwork
- Natural textiles

Greenery should be realistic and proportionate.

## Spatial Character

The room should feel:
- Natural
- Comfortable
- Warm
- Contemporary
- Soft
- Relaxed
- Sophisticated

## Avoid

- Rustic farmhouse styling
- Excessive rattan
- Excessive plants
- Tropical resort styling
- Bright colours
- Glossy finishes
- Overly sharp modernism
- Visual clutter

## Photorealism Direction

Natural materials should display realistic texture and variation.

Plants, fabrics, timber, stone and plaster should all respond naturally to the lighting.

Prioritise realistic contact shadows and believable material scale.
