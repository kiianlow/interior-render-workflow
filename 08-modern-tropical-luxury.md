# Style 08 — Modern Tropical Luxury

## Design Identity

Contemporary tropical residential interior adapted for a sophisticated Singapore home.

Combine modern architecture with warm natural materials, greenery, natural ventilation cues and indoor-outdoor visual continuity.

The result should feel like a refined tropical residence rather than a resort hotel.

## Colour Palette

Primary:
- Warm white
- Cream
- Sand
- Beige

Secondary:
- Natural teak
- Warm brown
- Stone
- Taupe

Accent:
- Deep green
- Olive
- Charcoal
- Muted terracotta

## Materials

Preferred:
- Natural timber
- Teak
- Travertine
- Limestone
- Natural stone
- Rattan used selectively
- Cane
- Linen
- Woven fabrics
- Textured plaster
- Terracotta ceramics
- Matte finishes

## Furniture Language

Use:
- Comfortable large sofas
- Natural timber furniture
- Woven accent chairs
- Sculptural coffee tables
- Relaxed dining chairs
- Indoor-outdoor furniture language
- Organic forms

Furniture should feel premium and comfortable.

## Lighting

Use:
- Strong but soft tropical daylight
- Natural window light
- Warm evening lighting
- Pendant lights using natural materials
- Warm architectural lighting

Daylight should feel bright but not overexposed.

## Curtains

Prefer:
- Lightweight linen
- Sheers
- Natural woven fabrics
- Warm white
- Beige

## Decorative Styling

Use:
- Realistic tropical greenery
- Large leafy plants
- Natural ceramics
- Timber objects
- Woven pieces
- Subtle tropical artwork

Greenery should support the architecture rather than dominate the room.

## Spatial Character

The room should feel:
- Tropical
- Luxurious
- Relaxed
- Natural
- Bright
- Resort-inspired
- Residential

## Avoid

- Excessive rattan
- Beach-house styling
- Strong tropical prints
- Resort-hotel appearance
- Excessive greenery
- Bright artificial colours
- Overly rustic materials
- Excessive bamboo

## Photorealism Direction

Use realistic tropical daylight, natural plant shadows, believable timber grain and physically accurate stone, fabric and glass.

Greenery must have realistic scale, density and lighting response.
