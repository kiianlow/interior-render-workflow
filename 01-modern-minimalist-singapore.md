# Style 01 — Modern Minimalist Singapore Condo

## Design Identity

Modern minimalist Singapore condominium interior.

The design should feel warm, refined, contemporary, practical and highly livable rather than cold or showroom-like.

Prioritise clean architectural lines, uncluttered spaces, warm neutrals, natural materials and carefully selected furniture.

The overall impression should be premium but understated.

## Colour Palette

Primary:
- Warm off-white
- Soft beige
- Warm taupe
- Light neutral grey

Secondary:
- Warm brown
- Natural timber tones
- Soft greige

Accent:
- Restrained charcoal
- Occasional muted bronze or brushed metal

Avoid excessive pure white, black or strong saturated colours.

## Materials

Preferred:
- Natural or warm-toned timber
- Light oak
- Walnut where deeper contrast is appropriate
- Travertine
- Natural stone
- Microcement
- Matte painted surfaces
- Linen
- Boucle
- Textured fabrics
- Clear or lightly tinted glass
- Brushed metal

Materials should have realistic texture, roughness, grain and subtle imperfections.

Avoid overly glossy or plastic-looking surfaces.

## Furniture Language

Use:
- Low-profile sofas
- Softly curved furniture
- Clean-lined dining tables
- Simple sculptural occasional chairs
- Minimal coffee tables
- Integrated-looking storage
- Contemporary dining chairs
- Simple upholstered beds

Furniture should feel appropriately scaled to a Singapore condominium.

Maintain comfortable circulation and realistic functional clearances.

## Lighting

Use:
- Soft natural daylight
- Warm architectural lighting
- Recessed downlights
- Subtle cove lighting
- Warm pendant lights
- Soft floor or table lamps where appropriate

Daylight should remain believable and should not overpower the room.

Avoid dramatic cinematic lighting unless specifically requested.

## Curtains and Window Treatment

Prefer:
- Full-height sheer curtains
- Warm neutral curtains
- Linen-look fabrics
- Simple floor-to-ceiling treatments

Keep window treatments elegant and visually quiet.

## Decorative Styling

Use restraint.

Suitable:
- Minimal artwork
- Ceramic objects
- Neutral cushions
- Sculptural vases
- Small amounts of greenery
- Coffee-table books
- Simple decorative objects

Do not fill empty surfaces unnecessarily.

## Spatial Character

The room should feel:
- Spacious
- Calm
- Warm
- Refined
- Contemporary
- Practical
- Expensive without appearing ostentatious

## Avoid

- Cold sterile minimalism
- Excessive black
- Excessive gold
- Excessive marble
- Heavy ornamentation
- Over-decoration
- Hotel-lobby appearance
- Excessive luxury detailing
- Oversized furniture
- Artificial CGI appearance
- Glossy plastic materials
- Unnecessary architectural changes

## Photorealism Direction

Use realistic architectural visualization quality.

Prioritise:
- Accurate scale
- Natural material behaviour
- Realistic shadows
- Realistic reflections
- Natural daylight
- Contact shadows
- Correct furniture proportions
- Physically believable surfaces
- Subtle material imperfections

The result should look like a professionally photographed completed Singapore condominium interior.
