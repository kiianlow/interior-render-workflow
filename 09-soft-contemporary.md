# Style 09 — Soft Contemporary

## Design Identity

A refined, comfortable contemporary interior with softened edges, warm neutrals and subtle luxury.

This style prioritises livability and comfort while maintaining a clean premium appearance.

## Colour Palette

Primary:
- Warm white
- Cream
- Beige
- Soft taupe

Secondary:
- Warm grey
- Light oak
- Walnut
- Greige

Accent:
- Muted brown
- Charcoal
- Soft bronze

## Materials

Preferred:
- Light oak
- Walnut
- Natural stone
- Travertine
- Linen
- Boucle
- Wool
- Matte finishes
- Soft plaster
- Brushed metal

## Furniture Language

Use:
- Rounded sofas
- Soft armchairs
- Rounded coffee tables
- Upholstered dining chairs
- Soft-edged cabinetry
- Comfortable beds
- Simple contemporary furniture

Furniture should feel inviting rather than overly sculptural.

## Lighting

Use:
- Soft natural daylight
- Warm recessed lighting
- Floor lamps
- Table lamps
- Soft pendant lighting
- Indirect lighting

The atmosphere should be warm and comfortable.

## Curtains

Prefer:
- Soft linen
- Warm white
- Cream
- Beige
- Full-height curtains

## Decorative Styling

Use:
- Soft artwork
- Neutral ceramics
- Books
- Cushions
- Throws
- Small plants
- Simple decorative objects

Decor should make the space feel lived-in without becoming cluttered.

## Spatial Character

The room should feel:
- Comfortable
- Warm
- Elegant
- Contemporary
- Relaxed
- Family-friendly
- Premium

## Avoid

- Excessive minimalism
- Excessive dark colours
- Hard industrial finishes
- Excessive ornamentation
- Cold white lighting
- Oversized furniture
- Hotel styling
- Excessive decorative objects

## Photorealism Direction

Prioritise believable residential comfort.

Show realistic fabric compression, cushions, upholstery, timber grain, soft shadows and natural daylight.

The final image should look like a professionally photographed real home.
