# Style 04 — Wabi-Sabi Natural Luxury

## Design Identity

Sophisticated wabi-sabi interior focused on natural materials, imperfect textures, earthy colours and quiet luxury.

The room should feel calm, organic and timeless.

Luxury comes from material quality and craftsmanship rather than decoration.

## Colour Palette

Primary:
- Warm ivory
- Sand
- Beige
- Taupe
- Stone

Secondary:
- Warm brown
- Clay
- Natural timber
- Soft greige

Accent:
- Charcoal
- Muted earthy tones

## Materials

Preferred:
- Travertine
- Limestone
- Natural stone
- Limewash
- Textured plaster
- Microcement
- Raw or lightly finished timber
- Linen
- Cotton
- Handmade ceramics
- Woven materials

Natural variation and subtle imperfections are desirable.

## Furniture Language

Use:
- Organic-shaped sofas
- Rounded tables
- Solid timber furniture
- Stone coffee tables
- Soft linen seating
- Simple upholstered beds
- Handmade-looking accent pieces

Furniture should appear substantial but not heavy.

## Lighting

Use:
- Warm natural daylight
- Diffused daylight
- Warm indirect lighting
- Soft pendant fixtures
- Subtle wall lights

Lighting should emphasise material texture.

## Curtains

Prefer:
- Natural linen
- Warm beige
- Cream
- Taupe

Use soft, slightly textured fabrics.

## Decorative Styling

Use:
- Handmade ceramics
- Sculptural stone objects
- Dried branches
- Organic artwork
- Minimal greenery
- Woven baskets
- Natural textiles

Avoid symmetrical decorative arrangements that feel overly staged.

## Spatial Character

The room should feel:
- Organic
- Calm
- Grounded
- Tactile
- Warm
- Timeless
- Quietly luxurious

## Avoid

- Perfectly polished surfaces everywhere
- Excessive marble
- Bright white
- Chrome-heavy finishes
- Excessive gold
- Artificial-looking plants
- Over-decoration
- Glossy furniture
- Strong contemporary showroom styling

## Photorealism Direction

Material texture is especially important.

Show:
- Stone variation
- Timber grain
- Fabric weave
- Plaster texture
- Natural imperfections
- Soft shadows
- Subtle reflections

The final render should feel authentic, tactile and naturally photographed.
