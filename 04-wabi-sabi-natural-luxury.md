# Style 04: Wabi-Sabi Natural Luxury

## Design Identity

Sophisticated wabi-sabi interior focused on natural materials, imperfect textures, earthy colours and quiet luxury.

The room should feel calm, organic and timeless.

Luxury comes from material quality and craftsmanship rather than decoration.

## Colour Palette

Primary:
- Warm ivory
- Sand
- Beige
- Taupe
- Stone

Secondary:
- Warm brown
- Clay
- Natural timber
- Soft greige

Accent:
- Charcoal
- Muted earthy tones

## Materials

Preferred:
- Travertine
- Limestone
- Natural stone
- Limewash
- Textured plaster
- Microcement
- Raw or lightly finished timber, such as natural white oak with visible grain
- Linen
- Cotton
- Handmade ceramics
- Woven materials
- Matte black or aged bronze metal, used sparingly

Natural variation and subtle imperfections are desirable.

## Furniture Language

Use:
- Organic-shaped sofas
- Rounded tables
- Solid timber furniture
- Stone coffee tables
- Soft linen seating
- Simple upholstered beds
- Handmade-looking accent pieces

Furniture should appear substantial but not heavy.

## Lighting

Use:
- Warm natural daylight
- Diffused daylight
- Warm indirect lighting
- Soft pendant fixtures
- Subtle wall lights

Lighting should emphasise material texture.

Recessed, cove and linear lights only where the ceiling already has a false ceiling (START-HERE section 7). Otherwise use pendants, surface ceiling lights, wall lights and lamps.

## Curtains

Prefer:
- Natural linen
- Warm beige
- Cream
- Taupe

Use soft, slightly textured fabrics.

## Wet Areas and Kitchen

- Bathroom floor and walls: Honed grey microcement-look tiles on floor and walls
- Bathroom fittings: White oak vanity, stone or stoneware basin, aged bronze or matte black tapware
- Kitchen: White oak flat-panel cabinets with a limewash-look finish, honed travertine-look counter

## Decorative Styling

Use:
- Handmade ceramics
- Sculptural stone objects
- Dried branches
- Organic artwork
- Minimal greenery
- Woven baskets
- Natural textiles

Avoid symmetrical decorative arrangements that feel overly staged.

## Spatial Character

The room should feel:
- Organic
- Calm
- Grounded
- Tactile
- Warm
- Timeless
- Quietly luxurious

## Avoid

- Perfectly polished surfaces everywhere
- Excessive marble
- Bright white
- Chrome-heavy finishes
- Excessive gold
- Artificial-looking plants
- Over-decoration
- Glossy furniture
- Strong contemporary showroom styling

## Photorealism Direction

Material texture is especially important.

Show:
- Stone variation
- Timber grain
- Fabric weave
- Plaster texture
- Natural imperfections
- Soft shadows
- Subtle reflections

The final render should feel authentic, tactile and naturally photographed.
