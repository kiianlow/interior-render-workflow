# Style 06 — Contemporary Luxury

## Design Identity

Modern high-end residential interior with a stronger architectural and visual presence.

The design should feel sleek, sophisticated and premium while remaining residential and functional.

## Colour Palette

Primary:
- Warm white
- Cream
- Taupe
- Greige

Secondary:
- Walnut
- Dark timber
- Warm grey
- Natural stone

Accent:
- Charcoal
- Black
- Brushed bronze
- Brushed stainless steel

## Materials

Preferred:
- Large-format natural stone
- Marble
- Travertine
- Walnut
- Dark oak
- Smoked glass
- Clear glass
- Brushed metal
- Premium upholstery
- Microcement

Use contrast between light and dark materials.

## Furniture Language

Use:
- Sculptural sofas
- Large low-profile seating
- Stone coffee tables
- Contemporary dining tables
- Architectural lounge chairs
- Refined built-in cabinetry
- Statement lighting fixtures

Furniture can be visually stronger than in the other styles but must remain correctly scaled.

## Lighting

Use:
- Natural daylight
- Recessed architectural lighting
- Linear lighting
- Indirect cove lighting
- Statement pendants
- Warm accent lighting

Use light to highlight architectural features and materials.

## Curtains

Prefer:
- Full-height curtains
- Warm neutral fabrics
- Sheers combined with heavier curtains where appropriate

## Decorative Styling

Use:
- Large-scale artwork
- Sculptural objects
- Premium ceramics
- Carefully selected books
- Controlled greenery
- Statement decorative pieces

## Spatial Character

The room should feel:
- Sleek
- Premium
- Sophisticated
- Architectural
- Contemporary
- Confident

## Avoid

- Excessive ornamentation
- Excessive gold
- Overly traditional furniture
- Cheap glossy surfaces
- Excessive black
- Excessive visual clutter
- Commercial showroom appearance

## Photorealism Direction

Maintain strong material realism.

Large stone surfaces should have believable veining and roughness.

Glass should have physically believable reflections.

Metal should show realistic brushed or polished behaviour.

Use natural shadows and realistic scale.
