# Negative Rules

Avoid, unless the user asks:

## Architecture and camera

- Any architectural change
- Wrong floorplan geometry or room proportions
- Added windows, doors or openings
- Distorted doors or windows
- Camera drift from the reference
- Exaggerated wide-angle distortion or leaning verticals

## Staging

- Floating furniture or missing contact shadows
- Furniture clipping through walls or blocking doors and windows
- Impossible circulation
- Wrong object scale
- Duplicate or deformed objects

## Look

- Plastic, CGI or heavy HDR appearance
- Excessive gloss
- Wrong reflections or shadows
- Over-decoration and generic luxury styling
- Random feature walls
- Overly dramatic lighting (Style 10 allows moodier light, still believable)
- Fireplaces, radiators and other cold-climate items
- Invented views such as sea, landmarks or skylines not stated by the user

## Image

- Text, labels, logos, signatures, watermarks
- AI artefacts

Never trade architecture for aesthetics.
