# Perspective Rules

## Reference photo supplied

The camera is part of the source. Preserve:

- Camera position and height
- Viewing direction and angle
- Field of view and lens character
- Framing and crop
- Relative object positions, scale and depth

Do not:

- Move the camera to make the room look bigger
- Switch to an exaggerated wide-angle lens
- Raise or lower the viewpoint without a reason
- Recompose the scene into a different view of the room

## Floorplan only

The camera is generated. State it in one line before generating, for example:
"Camera: from the main door looking toward the balcony, eye level 1.4 m, 24 mm equivalent, verticals straight."

Defaults:

- Eye level about 1.4 to 1.5 m
- Lens 20 to 24 mm full-frame equivalent, never wider than 16 mm
- Vertical lines stay vertical (real estate photography standard)
- Living and dining: from the entrance or a corner toward the windows or balcony
- Bedroom: from the doorway toward the window, bed at an angle
- Kitchen: from the kitchen entrance along the main counter run
- Bathroom: from the doorway, never showing more floor area than the room has

The generated camera must be a position a person could stand in, inside the room, consistent with the plan.
